Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Readlark ("the App") is developed by 0Quantum ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the App.

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We collect no data. Your books, notes, reading statistics and game progress stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Books you import, your reading position, quotes, notes, chapter recaps, reading statistics, settings and game progress are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (for example with the backup or share buttons), is not readable by us or any third party, and is deleted when you delete the App.

Reading aloud

Listening mode uses the text-to-speech voices built into your device. Text is spoken on the device by the operating system.

Reminders

Daily reading reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

PDF import

When you import a PDF, the App downloads the open-source PDF.js library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your IP address. The PDF itself is processed on your device and is never uploaded.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and does not ask for the advertising identifier.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
