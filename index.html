Common questions

Which formats can I read?

EPUB, FB2, MOBI (without DRM), PDF and plain text.

Where are my books and notes stored?

Only on your device. Use Notes → share → Backup to save a file, and Restore to bring it back on a new phone.

Does the App work offline?

Yes. Only importing a PDF needs a connection.

The voice sounds robotic.

The App uses your device's voices. On iPhone, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
